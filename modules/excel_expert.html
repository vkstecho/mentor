Excel Expert — Plant & Office

Practical Excel for packaging plants and office work: production logs, yield, manpower, and simple dashboards. Learn patterns you will reuse every week.

What you will cover

  • XLOOKUP / VLOOKUP for item & machine codes
  • Pivot tables for production by shift / SKU
  • Yield & waste formulas
  • Shift roster layout
  • Conditional formatting for alerts
  • Charts for daily KPIs

Lesson 1 — Clean data first

  • One header row only
  • Dates as real dates (not text)
  • Machine / SKU codes in a consistent format
  • No merged cells in the data block

Lesson 2 — Core formulas

Yield %     = Good_kg / Input_kg
Scrap cost  = Scrap_kg * Material_Rs_per_kg
OD OK?      = IF(AND(OD>=Min, OD<=Max), "OK", "NG")

XLOOKUP example:
=XLOOKUP(A2, Codes!A:A, Codes!B:B, "Not found")

Lesson 3 — Pivot for shift review

  • Rows: Machine or SKU
  • Columns: Shift (A/B/C)
  • Values: Sum of Good kg, Sum of Downtime min
  • Filter: Date range for the week

Lesson 4 — Conditional formatting

Highlight yield < target in red; OD out of band in orange; downtime > 30 min in bold. Managers should see problems without reading every row.
