Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Mentor without paying?

Yes. You can open the free learning modules without booking a paid mentorship session. Sign in if you want the app to track eligible learning progress.

What happens after I make a payment?

The booking request is submitted for mentor review. Access to the paid topic workspace is provided after the mentor approves the request. Keep your payment confirmation for reference.

Are sessions self-paced or one-to-one?

Free modules are self-paced resources. Paid guidance is booked as one-to-one mentor time, based on the topic and preferred time window.

Can I use Mentor on my phone?

Yes. Mentor is mobile-friendly and supports installation as a Progressive Web App on compatible browsers and devices.

Do I get a certificate?

The app supports digital completion certificates for eligible modules and learning paths. A certificate indicates completion of the learning content, not an accredited qualification.
