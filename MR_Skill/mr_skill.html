Department improvement

What management expects in a department review

Leadership wants a clear story with checkable numbers — not a data dump: where you stand, what improved, what slipped, and what you will do next.

Nine core improvement parameters

1
Output vs planActual production (MT / rolls / jobs) against the committed plan. State the gap in % and absolute units.
2
Yield / qualityGood output ÷ input. Scrap, B-grade, and customer complaints. Trend vs last month and vs target.
3
Conversion / process cost₹/kg or ₹/unit — wire, boat, power, wastage, manpower. Prefer Actual and Equivalent where mix changes.
4
Machine utilisation & downtimeRunning hours vs available hours. Top 3 stoppage reasons with hours and actions.
5
Safety & complianceIncidents, near-misses, 5S / audit scores. Zero-harm is non-negotiable.
6
People & skillAttendance, overtime, training completed, critical skill gaps on the line.
7
Customer & deliveryOn-time delivery %, quality complaints, claims. Link shop-floor actions to customer impact.
8
Actions from last reviewClosed / open / delayed. Management tracks whether commitments were kept.
9
Next-month prioritiesMaximum 3 focus items with owner and date. Vague lists lose attention.

Tip: Lead with the one number management cares about most (often cost, yield, or OTIF), support with 2–3 drivers, end with decisions you need from them.
